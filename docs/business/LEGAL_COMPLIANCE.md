# Legal & Compliance Register — Local Gig Marketplace

> **Status:** Living document. This file tracks business/legal/tax questions that require **professional legal and accounting review**. The engineering team does not make legal determinations; the platform is architected to remain flexible until counsel decides.
>
> Nothing in this repository constitutes legal advice or a legal position.

## How to use this register

Each item: what it is, why it matters, how the architecture stays flexible, and its review status. Items must be dispositioned by qualified professionals **before pilot launch** unless marked otherwise.

## L-1. Worker classification — CRITICAL

- **Issue:** Whether workers are independent contractors, employees, or another classification varies by jurisdiction (e.g., ABC tests, economic-reality tests) and shapes taxes, benefits, minimum pay, scheduling constraints, and platform liability.
- **Engineering posture:** The platform makes **no classification claims** in UI, docs, or contracts generated by us. Data model stores facts (jobs, hours, earnings) usable under any model. Fee/payout logic is configurable if the model changes.
- **Needs:** Employment counsel opinion for the pilot state **before launch**. Terms of service drafted by counsel.
- **Status:** OPEN.

## L-2. Terms of Service / Privacy Policy / Worker & Customer agreements

- **Issue:** Required contracts between platform↔customer, platform↔worker; arbitration/liability clauses; acceptable-use policy backing the restricted-work framework.
- **Engineering posture:** Onboarding has agreement-acceptance steps with versioned records (who accepted what, when). Documents themselves come from counsel.
- **Status:** OPEN — blocker for launch.

## L-3. Insurance & liability

- **Issue:** Property damage, worker injury, third-party injury during jobs. General liability / occupational-accident / category-specific coverage decisions.
- **Engineering posture:** Platform **does not advertise any insurance coverage** anywhere. Category records carry a `requires_insurance` flag so coverage-gated categories stay disabled until a real program exists. Internal stance recorded: *no coverage exists today*.
- **Needs:** Insurance broker/counsel review; decision before launch.
- **Status:** OPEN — blocker for launch.

## L-4. Background checks (FCRA et al.)

- **Issue:** Consumer-report laws (e.g., FCRA in the US) govern background checks: consent, adverse-action notices, data handling. State/local rules vary.
- **Engineering posture:** Verification framework supports a background-check type via provider adapter; **not wired**. Categories requiring it remain disabled. No check data stored beyond provider reference + pass/fail.
- **Status:** OPEN — required before enabling any category configured to need it.

## L-5. Payments, money transmission, and Stripe Connect terms

- **Issue:** Marketplace payment flows must fit within Stripe Connect's terms to avoid money-transmitter licensing questions; platform fee disclosures; refund policy enforceability.
- **Engineering posture:** Standard Stripe Connect marketplace pattern (ADR-004); no custodial wallets, no stored balances outside Stripe, no crypto.
- **Needs:** Counsel confirmation of flow + disclosures for pilot jurisdiction.
- **Status:** OPEN.

## L-6. Tax reporting

- **Issue:** Worker earnings reporting (e.g., US 1099-K/1099-NEC thresholds), platform tax obligations, possible marketplace-facilitator rules; regional differences.
- **Engineering posture:** Immutable financial ledger retains all data needed for any reporting regime (gross/fees/net/parties/periods). Stripe's Connect tax-form tooling is a candidate. **No tax features implemented without professional review.**
- **Status:** OPEN — must be resolved before first tax year closes with real earnings.

## L-7. Minors / age requirements

- **Issue:** Labor-law age restrictions vary by work type and jurisdiction.
- **Engineering posture:** Platform-wide minimum age (18 assumed pending review) enforced at signup; per-category `min_worker_age` supported.
- **Status:** OPEN — confirm platform minimum + per-category ages with counsel.

## L-8. Restricted/regulated work catalog

- **Issue:** Which job types are prohibited (licensed trades, childcare, medical, passenger transport, hazardous work, etc.) and what screening is legally expected of the platform.
- **Engineering posture:** Categories default-disabled until reviewed; `restricted_terms` screening + admin review queue; every category enablement should follow a legal checklist.
- **Status:** OPEN — initial allowed-category list needs counsel sign-off for pilot market.

## L-9. Privacy / data-protection law

- **Issue:** State privacy laws (e.g., CCPA/CPRA if California), deletion/export rights, retention schedules, minors' data, location-data rules.
- **Engineering posture:** Data minimization, deletion/anonymization path, retention limits on location snapshots, PII access controls (see SECURITY_MODEL, DATABASE_SCHEMA §privacy). Formal retention schedule needs counsel input.
- **Status:** OPEN.

## L-10. Anti-discrimination & fair matching

- **Issue:** Matching/ranking must not discriminate on protected characteristics; some jurisdictions scrutinize algorithmic work allocation.
- **Engineering posture:** Documented deterministic matching policy; protected characteristics are never model inputs; policy changes require an ADR/DECISIONS entry (audit trail).
- **Status:** OPEN — periodic review once live.

## L-11. Communications compliance

- **Issue:** SMS consent (e.g., TCPA in the US), marketing vs transactional email rules (CAN-SPAM), push-notification consent.
- **Engineering posture:** Explicit opt-ins per channel/category; transactional vs marketing separated in `notification_preferences`; SMS strictly transactional at launch.
- **Status:** OPEN — confirm consent copy with counsel.

## L-12. Accessibility obligations

- **Issue:** ADA/WCAG expectations for consumer apps.
- **Engineering posture:** Accessibility requirements built into UI standards (PRD §31).
- **Status:** TRACKING.

## Pre-launch legal gate (summary)

Blockers before pilot: L-1 (classification opinion), L-2 (ToS/Privacy/agreements), L-3 (insurance decision), L-5 (payment-flow confirmation), L-7 (age policy), L-8 (category list sign-off). The ROADMAP's Phase 19 (pilot deployment) is gated on this list.
